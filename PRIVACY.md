# CHIONA CHICKEN FARM – privacy

- Your farm records (flocks, eggs, vaccines, money, incubator batches) are stored on your phone.
- If you turn on sharing, the records are also copied to the Firebase Realtime Database that **you** create and own. Only people who hold your farm code can read or change that copy. The app has no other server and no analytics or ads.
- The app asks for notification permission (daily reminder) and internet access (sharing only).
- Anyone with the farm code can join your farm, so share it only with people you trust.
- Remove the app and clear its data to delete the records on a phone. Delete the database in your Firebase console to delete the shared copy.
